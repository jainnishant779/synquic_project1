# R7 brief: legal and platform constraints (as of 2026-11-01)

All conclusions are [COUNSEL]: research for lawyer review, not legal advice.

## Recommendation

Build Social 360 on the assumption that **no platform API data enters the shared base, for any brand, on any platform**. Per-brand adapters should train mainly on brand first-party material obtained outside platform APIs. Generate synthetic data only with Apache or MIT open-weight models, never hosted LLM APIs. Treat Synquic as an AI Act **provider**, not only a deployer, and ship marking with the video engine.

## What constrains the architecture

| Data source | Shared base | Per-brand adapter |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed or public-domain corpora with clear rights, personal data screened | Yes, subject to rights check | Yes |
| Brand first-party copy held outside the platform (CMS, DAM, master files), under contract | Only with explicit opt-in; GDPR controller role follows | Yes, processor role on instruction |
| Brand data pulled through platform APIs | No (R7-002, 005, 008, 017) | Meta and YouTube Conditional (R7-001, 016); X and LinkedIn No (R7-005, 008); Pinterest Unclear (R7-022); TikTok, Reddit unverifiable |
| Public third-party platform data | No: X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest (R7-006, 009, 014, 021). Meta Unclear, plan as No (R7-003). TikTok, Reddit unverifiable, plan as No | Same |
| Hosted LLM outputs as synthetic data | Blocked (R7-023, 025, 026, 027, 028, 029) | Blocked |
| Apache or MIT model outputs | Allowed (R7-033, 034, 035) | Allowed |

The X, LinkedIn and Meta bars have no own-data carve-out, and derived or anonymised data stays inside the definitions (R7-003, 007, 010). So use API engagement data for live reporting only, kept per tenant, and do not train on it unless the platform agrees in writing.

## Key findings

**Platform terms.** Only X (R7-005, 006) and Pinterest (R7-021) have express AI clauses. Meta, LinkedIn and YouTube reach the same place through purpose limits, so those Nos are inferences. Meta requires Tech Providers to process only for that Client and to keep Client data separate (R7-001, 004). YouTube bars derived data and cross-owner aggregation, caps stored data at 30 days, and bars storing audiovisual content without approval, which also limits video engine training (R7-014, 015, 016, 017).

**LLM terms.** Anthropic's Usage Policy bars training any AI model without authorization (R7-026). Google Cloud's test is "similar", not "competing" (R7-028). OpenAI's classifier exception does not clearly cover anything sold to customers (R7-024). Llama adds naming duties and an EU multimodal restriction; older Gemma terms pass down to the trained model (R7-030, 031, 032).

**Privacy.** On a brand's instructions Synquic is plausibly a processor for its adapter; pooling brand personal data into a base makes it a controller (R7-040, 041). Presume adapter weights hold personal data, so deletion must reach them (R7-036, 047). Legitimate interest needs a documented test (R7-037), and a tainted base spreads risk to all tenants (R7-038). The GDPR Omnibus is not law (R7-043). California is more permissive (R7-046). India exempts self-published data; substantive duties are reported for May 2027 (R7-049, 051). Plan a DPIA (R7-042).

**EU AI Act.** Art. 50 has applied since 2 August 2026 (R7-054). A system launched now gets no grace to 2 December 2026 (R7-055). Fine-tuning with new data under Synquic's name makes it a system provider (R7-059). The GPAI threshold is modification compute above a third of the original, with fallbacks (R7-065, 066), so ordinary adapters should stay under it, while training a base above 10^23 FLOP makes Synquic a GPAI provider (R7-067). High-risk dates moved to 2027 and 2028 (R7-056). Logging FLOP per run is cheap evidence.

**Synthetic people.** A made-up realistic person can still be a deep fake (R7-060), and synthetic influencer ads are a listed non-exempt example (R7-061). New York requires disclosure of exactly these unrecognisable performers (R7-070). The FTC has no avatar ban, but fake testimonials are unlawful (R7-068). Add a disclosure default, C2PA plus a watermark (R7-077), and no fake customer testimonials. Do not warrant ownership of raw outputs (R7-080, 081). The NCII prohibition starts 2 December 2026 (R7-057).

**SOC 2 and DPAs.** All figures are vendor-marketing secondary and conflict: Type 1 about 1 to 6 months, Type 2 about 6 to 15 (R7-082, 083); small-firm auditor fees 7.5K to 20K USD, about 25K USD all-in at the low end (R7-084, 085, 086). Practical path: Type 1 for first enterprise deals, then a 3 to 6 month Type 2 window. Microsoft and Adobe set the benchmark of customer-exclusive fine-tunes (R7-087, 088). Expect isolation, no-training and deletion of weights as DPA terms (R7-089).

## Unknowns and gaps

- **TikTok and Reddit terms were unreadable** (R7-012, 013, 019, 020, 076). This is the largest gap in the highest-priority question.
- Unread primaries: OpenAI policy pages, ELVIS Act, California AB 2013 and SB 942, DPDP Rules, EUR-Lex text of Regulation 2026/1744, UK legislation, ICO generative AI position, X Master License, Pinterest API Terms, Meta Developer Docs, Code of Practice, NO FAKES text.
- Meta's labelling of third-party AI ads conflicts between its own pages (R7-073, 074).
- No real brand DPA was seen, so R7-089 is a hypothesis. NO FAKES status is stale after June 2026. Cross-border transfers and copyright in training data were not covered.

## What would change the answer

- A written data licence from a platform opens that platform for training.
- If counsel reads "foundation or frontier model" narrowly, small classifiers on X data may be open.
- If TikTok or Reddit terms permit adapter use on a customer's own data, the matrix widens.
- Adoption of the GDPR Omnibus eases the basis, not platform terms.
- A shared base trained from scratch above 10^23 FLOP, or Synquic's EU seat, changes the provider duties and Llama eligibility.
