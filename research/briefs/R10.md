## Findings that matter

**TTS.** Eleven v4 Turbo leads the Artificial Analysis arena (1336), then Eleven v4 (1334), Qwen-Audio-3.1 (1305), Cartesia Sonic 3.6 (1296), Gemini 3.8 Flash (1283). The arena is not Hindi-specific, has no page date, and OpenAI, Sarvam, Azure and Amazon were not in the top 15 (011-016). I found no independent Hindi or Hinglish TTS ranking (017). The only Hinglish TTS evidence is Sarvam's own blind test with Josh Talks (20,000+ votes): ElevenLabs v3 alpha led on general quality, Bulbul V3 beat Cartesia Sonic-3 (037). Independent ASR data favours Sarvam on spontaneous Hindi (15.29 WER vs Scribe-v2 27.46) but Scribe-v2 wins on read code-switch (7.57 vs 16.47) (049, 050).
- Sarvam has only 11 languages and bars standalone resale of audio Output and resale of API access (036, 038). A narrated video is probably not standalone audio, but this is untested: Counsel.
- Cartesia and Sarvam both train on or may keep customer content unless negotiated (023). Cartesia Pro+ needed for commercial use (021).
- Unverifiable: OpenAI terms (403), Google Cloud TTS and Azure prices (JS pages) (027, 031, 032). OpenAI tts-1 is $15/1M chars (025). Polly Neural $16/1M (033).

**Music.** ElevenLabs allows web and social commercial use but bars film, TV, radio; limits Scale to under 10 and Business to under 50 employees; prohibits Model Resale and Music Library creation below Enterprise (057-059). Lyria 3.5 is $0.08/song but the docs state no output licence; falls to Gemini API terms (061, 062, 030). Stable Audio: $0.08, Community License under $1M revenue (063, 064). Suno consumer terms bar reselling the Service and have no API: Blocked. Udio unverifiable (065, 066). Artlist 403 (070). Soundstripe API is Enterprise only (068).

**Avatars.** Only Tavus, Hedra, Synthesia (credits) and fal models gave readable prices. HeyGen's price page is JS-only; its terms bar white-labeling and give HeyGen a training licence (075, 076). Tavus allows enterprise white-label of the consent flow (081). No avatar vendor showed Hindi lip-sync or 9:16 support on its pricing page.

**Lip-sync.** sync.so $0.04-0.133/s by model (090). sync.so terms bar repackaging output in API form and grant no explicit commercial-use clause (091). LatentSync: code Apache-2.0, weights OpenRAIL++ (Counsel), and a pinned insightface dependency whose pretrained models are non-commercial (096). Use the fal-hosted route or counsel review before self-hosting.

**Captions.** Whisper-large-v3 Hindi WER is about 33-34 in two secondary sources (053). Voice of India (April 2026) Hindi OI-WER: Sarvam Audio 5.0, Gemini 3 Pro 6.0, Saarika 2.5 6.2 (048).

## What would change the answer
- A blind Hinglish listening test where Sarvam beats ElevenLabs v4 Turbo would make Sarvam primary despite its resale clause; if Sarvam's counsel reading fails, ElevenLabs or Gemini stay.
- Epidemic quoting a per-render price near $0.08 would beat Lyria on rights certainty; ElevenLabs Enterprise Music would be needed if end customers run on more than 50 staff.
- Gemini TTS 2027 doubling ($12/1M audio tokens Flash-Lite) narrows its cost edge.
- A readable HeyGen API rate or a Hedra Character-3 Hindi test could displace Tavus.
- Counsel clearing insightface use would allow self-hosted LatentSync at GPU cost only.
