# R2 brief: open-weight video models, as of 2026-11-01

Research, not legal advice. Verdicts assume a commercial SaaS with global customers, including US and EU, and under USD 10M revenue. Synquic's domicile was not given to me.

## Recommendation

Do not build the core product on the two strongest open models without a signed licence. Make **Wan 2.2** (Apache 2.0) the clean default for silent video, and open a commercial conversation with **Lightricks** now, because LTX-2.5 has the best spec but a clause aimed at products like Synquic's. Treat **MiniMax H3** as unavailable unless MiniMax grants a written licence. Evaluate **daVinci-MagiHuman**, **NVIDIA Cosmos3** and **Prism** as permissive audio-capable options.

**Hidden traps.** HF `apache-2.0` tags on H3 LoRAs do not override the H3 licence (R2-008). Using H3 outputs to train Synquic's own model is barred (R2-005). LTX marketing says "permissive" and "ARR"; the licence says neither (R2-025, 026). Pinning LTX-2.3 does not avoid the competition clause (R2-031).

**Superseded.** Wan core weights stop at 2.2; SEO claims of Apache Wan 2.7 or 3.0 are contradicted by the Wan-AI HF listing, though only 10 of 27 repos were displayed (R2-058). Mochi 1 and CogVideoX are not contenders (R2-090, 091). No HunyuanVideo 2.0 was found. HF search for HappyHorse returned no repo.

## Unknowns and gaps

- No single-GPU, 720p or 1080p LTX-2.5 render figure exists from any source I found. MOVA publishes step time only (R2-081). Prism publishes none (R2-089). NAVA and Cosmos3 conditions are incomplete (R2-078, 087).
- Wan table frames and steps are repo defaults, not stated in the table (R2-049).
- LTX VRAM conflict: 16 GB against 32 GB, same vendor (R2-034, 035). No official H3 VRAM figure (R2-013). LTX-2.5 hard clip limit unpublished (R2-039).
- Not read: Skywork licence PDF, LTX Acceptable Use Policy PDF, Gemma terms, Glyph-SDXL-v2 licence, MOVA LICENSE, Kandinsky HF gate conditions. Kandinsky VAE provenance and current Sber sanctions status are open.
- Coverage is 10 main models plus 3 brief entries. Attributes for smaller models sit in `conditions` and `notes`, not separate rows. FastWan, LightX2V and Wan-Animate-2 speeds were not captured.
- 93 facts, above the 80 target, because licence traps required extra rows.

## What would change the answer

- Lightricks confirming in writing that Synquic does not compete, or quoting a licence: LTX-2.5 becomes the lead model.
- MiniMax granting a licence, or widening the territory, which its Q&A signals as possible: H3 returns, subject to UI branding and its copyright litigation (R2-022).
- Synquic being outside all excluded territories and serving no one inside them: H3 and Hunyuan move to Counsel.
- A Synquic benchmark on rented GPUs, which should replace all figures above before the cost model is final.
