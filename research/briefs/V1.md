## What blocks a recommendation
1. **No lip-sync or identity chain in the report is commercially clear as shipped.** InsightFace sits inside the QC identity gate, LivePortrait, LatentSync, Hallo2 and FaceID. Either obtain an InsightFace licence or replace and re-test it. MuseTalk alone looks usable, subject to component checks.
2. **XTTS-v2 cannot voice paid videos.** Use a paid TTS API.
3. **Keyframes:** FLUX.1 schnell, a paid BFL licence or an API. Identity conditioning needs a new design.
4. **Open-weight default:** only Wan 2.1, Wan 2.2 and Mochi 1 are clean among the report's models, and Mochi is superseded. LTX is Counsel; Wan 3.0 is API only.
5. **R3's lip-sync default (fal LatentSync):** ask fal who licenses the InsightFace models before relying on the endpoint's commercial tag.
6. **Render times:** nothing beyond vendor claims is verified. Run a benchmark before the cost model is final.
7. **H3:** Comfy claims to resell a commercial licence, unconfirmed by MiniMax, territory unknown (V-033). Ask, since it could change R2's verdict.
8. **Gemini API** bars developing competing models (V-040). Add it to counsel's list with Alibaba's clause.
