# R1 brief: paid video generation APIs, as of 2026-11-01

## Recommendation

Do not build on a single vendor. Start with **MiniMax H3 direct** for volume and **Wan 3.0** for 30-second clips, and add **Gemini Omni Flash 1.1** as the GA fallback from a large vendor. Reach **Kling 4.0** and **Seedance 2.5** through Replicate, fal or Runway until the direct contracts are checked. MiniMax H3's API terms were unreadable, so read them before committing (R1-087). Do not plan around Sora or the Gemini API Veo IDs: both are gone.

## Key findings

**Shut down or at risk**
- Sora 2 and the Videos API were due to end 2026-09-24 with no replacement listed, and OpenAI's pricing page now shows no video model (R1-001, R1-002).
- Veo 3.1 preview IDs on the Gemini API were shut down 2026-10-22 (R1-020). Direct Veo now means Agent Platform, where GA models show retirement "November 17, 2026 or later", 16 days from the as-of date (R1-021).
- Pika now also resells Seedance, Omni, Wan and H3 (R1-121).

**Limits.** Best published: MiniMax H3 300 RPM and 30 in flight (R1-082, 083), Wan 300 RPM (R1-094), xAI 10 to 158 RPS by tier (R1-102). Runway Tier 1 allows 1 to 2 concurrent and a $100 monthly cap (R1-048, 049). Longest single clips: Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5 and Wan 3.0 at 30 seconds (R1-059, 071, 091); Veo 8 seconds (R1-024).

**Traps for the cost model**
- Promos: Kling 4.0 on Runway is 20% off until 2026-11-30 (R1-042); Wan 3.0 has a 30% label with no discounted figure published (R1-088).
- Audio: doubles Veo on fal at 720p and 1080p (R1-033); Kling 3.0 on fal defaults to audio on at $0.168 vs $0.112 (R1-063). It is free on Seedance on fal (R1-071).
- Video input is billed: Kling 4.0 on Replicate bills a 5s edit as 10 seconds (R1-058); Seedance counts input seconds in tokens (R1-066); H3 Max input costs more than output (R1-078).
- Upscaling: Omni 1080p and 4K are upscaled (R1-006); Grok Lite 1080p likewise (R1-100).
- Per clip: Luma and legacy Hailuo price per clip (R1-079, 105).
- Floors: 80 credits per Seedance 2.5 generation on Runway (R1-044).
- Failed jobs: Runway keeps credits on `SAFETY.INPUT` failures (R1-050). MiniMax, Alibaba, BytePlus, Luma and Replicate do not bill failures (R1-084, 095, 068, 111, 117). fal covers server errors only (R1-115).

**Legal flags (not legal advice)**
- Alibaba bars using Output to train competing products and bars resale of the service. Both matter for a company building its own engine (R1-096). Verdict: Counsel.
- Luma grants commercial rights only for Output made under an active paid subscription (R1-112).
- BytePlus's availability list omits the US (dated April 2026, Seedance not named; R1-069).
