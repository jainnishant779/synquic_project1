# R3 brief: rented GPU and pipeline unit prices, as of 2026-11-01

## Recommendation
- **GPU default: RunPod Secure Cloud Pods.** Per-second billing, no egress fees, public DPA, SOC 2 Type II (R3-003, 022, 026, 027, 028). L40S is $1.09/hr against $1.861/hr on AWS g6e.xlarge; H100 SXM is $3.99/hr against $6.88/hr on AWS p5.4xlarge (R3-088, 084).
- **Keep tenant data off cheap tiers.** RunPod Community is peer-to-peer and closed to new hosts (R3-029). Vast's SOC 2 and DPA do not establish that its hosts are audited or covered (R3-054, 055).
- **Do not budget on spot.** Treat interruptible as checkpointed batch upside only.
- **Pipeline defaults, pending a quality test:** Haiku 5.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite or gpt-5-nano for planning; FLUX 3 Klein for keyframes; ElevenLabs Flash or Cartesia for TTS; R2 for storage and delivery; fal LatentSync for clips over 40 s, sync.so otherwise.
- **Music is a legal decision first.** Both verdicts are Counsel (R3-121, 123).

## Key findings
**GPU traps**
- RunPod spot is retired and savings plans are closed (R3-012, 011).
- Stopped RunPod volume disks cost double, $0.20 against $0.10/GB/month. Network volumes stay at $0.07 (R3-024, 025).
- Vast bills storage while stopped and charges bandwidth both ways (R3-050, 051).
- AWS p5.48xlarge spot in us-east-1 is $57.76, above on-demand at $55.04; us-west-2 is $31.30 (R3-089, 085).
- Modal GPU Functions cannot be made non-preemptible, and GPU rates exclude CPU and memory (R3-071, 067).
- Together preemptible H100 is $1.99, reclaimable any time with no notice stated (R3-078).
- CoreWeave prices are per 8-GPU instance, not per GPU (R3-056).
- Lambda bills per minute, sells 80 GB A100 only as 8x, and lists no RTX 4090, L40S or H200 (R3-040, 037, 038).

**Serverless**
- RunPod's pricing page and docs disagree. H100 is $5.39/hr on the page and $0.00116/s in the docs (R3-013 to 018). Use the dated pricing page until RunPod confirms.
- Sub-200ms and "about one second" are boot claims, not model-load times, and RunPod bills cold start and idle timeout (R3-020, 021, 072). Measure with a real video model.

**Pipeline**
- TTS: ElevenLabs API is $0.04 to $0.08 per 1K characters; its credit-per-character figure is an unresolved "0.5 and 1" range (R3-112, 113, 115). Cartesia publishes only credits: 1 per character, $49 for 1.25M, overage $38 to $65 per 1M (R3-116 to 118). OpenAI tts-1 is $15.00 per 1M characters (R3-119).
- Images at 1K: FLUX 3 Klein $0.009, Nano Banana 2.1 $0.0336, FLUX 3 Image $0.048, GPT Image 2 medium $0.053 at 1024x1024 (R3-110, 107, 109, 108).
- LLM input/output per 1M: gpt-5-nano $0.05/$0.40, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite $0.10/$0.40, Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50, Together GLM-5.3-Flash $0.15/$0.50. DeepSeek flash is $0.30/$1.20 at peak, half that off-peak (R3-100 to 106, 098, 099, 136).
- Music: ElevenLabs is $0.15/min, Lyria 3.5 is $0.08 per song (R3-120, 122). ElevenLabs limits plans by headcount (Scale under 10, Business under 50 employees), excludes film, TV, radio and Studio Games below Enterprise Music, and bars Music Library and Model Resale (R3-121). Included minutes conflict: 1,993 against 1,100 on Scale (R3-134, 135).
- Lip-sync: sync.so is $0.04 to $0.133/s by model (R3-126, 127). fal LatentSync is $0.005/s only past 40 s, with a flat $0.20 below that (R3-129).
- Storage: R2 is $0.015/GB-month with free egress; S3 is $0.023 plus $0.09/GB egress; CloudFront is $0.085/GB after 1 TB free (R3-130, 131, 132, 094, 133).

## Unknowns and gaps
- **Vast:** only RTX 4090 has prices, from one snapshot. L40S, A100, H100 and H200 are unverifiable because the offers API returned 403 and I did not work around it (R3-049). The raw snapshot was lost when the shared scratchpad was cleared, so R3-045 to 048 cannot be re-checked by quote.
- **GCP:** unverifiable (R3-097). AWS is the only hyperscaler baseline.
- **Not established:** CoreWeave spot terms (login wall, R3-064); CoreWeave and Together DPAs (R3-066, 083); Lambda has no public DPA, and its filesystem rate is only an example (R3-044, 041); Together egress.
- **SOC 2:** every status is a vendor statement. No report was read. Together's source is from July 2025.
- **Licences not read:** Stable Audio, Soundraw, sync.so, FLUX API output, LatentSync model. DeepSeek direct-API data terms were not reviewed.
- **Quote checking:** 114 quotes matched raw page captures. 15 could not be: four Vast offers, plus pages seen only through the fetch tool (Gemini pricing and terms, AWS SOC, GDPR and spot-notice, Lambda and CoreWeave trust). Some table figures rest on the fetch render and say so in `notes`.
- **Volatility:** secondary trackers report October 2026 GPU price rises. Not verified, but treat this as a one-day snapshot.

## What would change the answer
- A measured video-model cold start under about a minute would favour serverless over always-on Pods.
- RunPod confirming the docs rates (R3-014, 016, 018) would lower serverless cost materially.
- Sustained GPU use would make a reserved quote matter. Only Together and AWS publish reserved rates (R3-079, 090).
- Customer-facing model access, or customers using output in film or TV, would likely push music to Enterprise terms or Lyria.
- If quality forces sync-3 or FLUX 3 Image at 4K, unit costs rise sharply (R3-127, 109).
