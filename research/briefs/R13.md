# R13 brief: free tiers and free credits for testing video, image and audio APIs

As of 2026-11-02. 72 facts in `raw/R13.jsonl`, all single_source or unverifiable (none verified by V). Figures come from the fetch tool's text extraction of vendor pages, not raw HTML. Treat the quotes as extraction wording and have V re-check any fact you will spend money on.

## Bottom line

Almost nothing is free through the API. Only three offers could cover a video test for $0 from a script: Alibaba Wan 3.0 (30 seconds, Singapore), Gemini TTS (audio only), and possibly BytePlus 500k tokens (model coverage unclear). Everything else needs a top-up. Google's free tier does not cover any current video or image model. Cheapest real top-ups: xAI (auto top-up floor $5), fal, Runway, Pruna ($0.01 to $0.02 per second video), FASHN $7.50, Together $15.

## What can we test for $0

1. **Alibaba wan3.0-video**, 30 s combined input plus output, Singapore International only, 90 days from activation, failed calls do not use quota (015, 016, 022). Turn on Free Quota Only to cap spend (021). The page lists wan3.0 prime and all other Wan models as having no free quota (017, 020). Re-check the console; the removal list changed on 2026-10-29.
2. **Gemini TTS** (audio), free of charge, with training-data caveat (035, 034).
3. **HeyGen Voice Turbo Free** (speech), until 2026-11-30 (027).
4. **ElevenLabs Free plan** 10,000 credits, treat as non-commercial (069, 070).
5. **Sarvam signup credits**, amount unknown (071). **Pixabay** stock search only (072).
6. **BytePlus 500k tokens**, only if the console shows it covers Seedance (044, 045). Unconfirmed.

Nothing for images or video other than Wan 3.0 is free at the API level. Gemini image and video models are explicitly "Not available" on the Free Tier (029, 032).

## Conflicts and gaps

- fal promo credit expiry: FAQ 1 week to 1 year vs ToS 90 days (003, 004).
- Blocked or unreadable: Kling (all pages JS shell, 043); HeyGen pricing pages (title only, 024); BytePlus free-trial page and docs 1099320 (empty or nav only, 045); Meta Muse page 404 (068); ElevenLabs help article 403 (070); BFL FLUX 3 price tables blank (040); first Wan pricing page fetch read only 100k of 143k characters (015).
- Not found on any page: minimum top-up for fal, MiniMax, Runway, xAI initial purchase, BFL, Replicate; card or phone requirement for nearly all providers. These are unknowns, not "no".
- xAI free credits: secondary reports conflict and say $25 and $150/month offers ended; not confirmed on xAI pages (037).
- Commercial rights of free outputs were stated by almost nobody. Assume test-only unless a vendor states otherwise.
- Not covered: Google Vertex AI free trial, Veo on Vertex, Kling third-party resellers, Recraft free-plan API access, Seedance 2.5 token-to-second conversion.
