# R8 brief: video generation models, quality evidence and cheapest options, as of 2026-11-01

Facts are in `raw/R8.jsonl` (114 facts, all `single_source` or `conflict`; none verified). Extends R1, R2 and R4; their facts are cited as R1-xxx and so on. Research, not legal advice. Quote text came through WebFetch's model-rendered pages, so V should spot-check table rows.

## Recommendations

**1. Hero shots: MiniMax H3 at 768p, with Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 as the non-Chinese equivalent.** H3 is top-6 on Artificial Analysis (AA) text-to-video at 1161 (R8-003) and top-5 on image-to-video at 1128 (R8-025). It is first on the arena.ai image-to-video board at 1495 with 57,112 votes (R8-043). MiniMax lists 0.08 USD/s at 768P (R1-074), and AA's own $4.80/min matches. Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 is first on arena.ai text-to-video (1516, only 1,784 votes, R8-035) but 1128 and 1046 on AA (R8-010, R8-031), at about 0.10 USD/s (R1-003). It is GA, and paid-tier prompts are not used for training (R8-089). Its clip limit is 10 seconds (R1-005). H3 carries three legal flags: MiniMax API terms were unreadable (R1-087), the weights licence bars the US and EU (R2-002 to R2-005), and audio output is unconfirmed (R1-086).

**2. Long text-to-video shots up to 30 seconds: Wan 3.0.** It leads AA text-to-video at 1175 (R8-001) and is rank 6 on arena.ai (1476, R8-039). At 720p it costs 0.10 USD/s on Alibaba, fal and WaveSpeed (R8-072, R8-073, R1-088). It supports 9:16 and 2 to 30 s clips (R8-072). It is weaker at animating a supplied still: 1111 on AA image-to-video (R8-028). Alibaba's clauses on competing products and resale need Counsel (R1-096). AA benchmarked it at 1080p (0.20 USD/s), so its Elo is not a 720p score.

**3. B-roll: a cheap tier, with Grok Imagine 1.5 Lite now and HeyGen Video 1 once its price is confirmed.** See the next section.

## Cheapest acceptable option per shot group

No yield data exists (R4-059 to R4-067), so these are price per generated second, not per usable second.

- **B-roll or filler: Grok Imagine 1.5 Lite at 0.020 USD/s** on xAI's models page (R8-091). It scores 991 on AA text-to-video (R8-017) and 999 on image-to-video (R8-032), level with the Kling 3.0 Pro anchor of 1000 (R8-016). 1080p is upscaled (R1-100). Two conflicts: AA's own price for it is $8.40/min (R8-017), and xAI's guide says resolution changes cost (R8-090). xAI's terms were never read (R1-103). **Likely better:** HeyGen Video 1 is 1156 and 1120 on AA (R8-006, R8-026) at 0.02 to 0.03 USD/s for 768p, with native 9:16, audio and 5 to 30 s clips (R8-067, R8-068). HeyGen's own pages disagree on price, the 50% promo ended 2026-10-31 (R8-069), and 1080p is 3x the 768p rate. **Third option:** PixVerse V6 on fal, 0.045 USD/s at 720p silent (R8-075), scoring 984 and 959.
- **Hero: MiniMax H3 at 0.08 USD/s (768P)**; 2K is 0.13 (R1-075). The 768p to 1080p step needs an upscale or the 2K tier. Non-Chinese hero: Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 at 0.10 or FLUX 3 Video HD at 0.17 USD/s (R8-053).

## Findings that change earlier briefs

- **Quality has moved.** The top 10 on AA is Wan 3.0, Utopai X (no API, R8-095), H3, Seedance 2.5, FLUX 3, HeyGen, Seedance 2.0 and Omni. Veo 3.1, LTX-2.5, Vidu Q3 and Runway Gen-4.5 are far below (R8-019, R8-020, R8-021, R8-042).
- **The two arenas disagree.** Grok 1.5 is fourth on arena.ai (R8-037) and 12th to 14th on AA (R8-013). Omni is first on arena.ai and 9th to 11th on AA. Use rank bands, not single Elo values.
- **Cheapest H3 route is encumbered.** Pruna's P-Video-2-Pro (0.045 to 0.095 at 1080p, R8-062) inherits the H3 Community License by contract (R8-064).
- **New retirements:** Veo 3.0 and 2.0 stable IDs on 2026-06-30 at the earliest (R8-087); Runway Gen-3 Alpha Turbo and Gen-4 Aleph on 2026-07-30 (secondary, R8-086). Resellers retire sooner than vendors (R8-088).
- **Open weights:** only MAGI-2 Preview is new. It is Apache-2.0 on the weights but needs 8 Hopper GPUs, gives 10 s clips only, and its text encoder and audio VAE licences are unread (R8-098 to R8-100). Not a cost-first pick.
- **Reselling traps:** the fal pricing page lists Grok 1.5 at 0.01 and PixVerse V6 at 0.005 USD/s, against 0.08 to 0.25 and 0.045 on the model pages (R8-079, R8-075). Together lists per-video prices with no duration (R8-080).
- **Terms:** BFL, Pruna and fal have no vendor indemnity for outputs (R8-060, R8-065, R8-083). Replicate deletes data after one hour (R8-082).

## Unknowns

- **Unverifiable:** Kling direct pricing and notices (R8-092), LTX direct pricing (R8-093), VBench scores (R8-050), HeyGen's API pricing page, MiniMax and HeyGen API terms.
- **Not found:** prices for Agnes-Video-2.5, Hyperflow H3, Boreal-H3 on AA, Utopai X outside PAI; origins for Vidu, PixVerse, Creatify, Sand AI (R8-114).
- **AA caveats:** no update date on the page; the image-to-video board does not say whether audio is on; prices and Elo are at the benchmarked resolution, usually 1080p.
- **Not covered:** rate limits for most new vendors, and whether failed jobs are billed (only Creatify refunds, R8-070). Luma Ray3.2, Runway Gen-4.5 specs, Hailuo 2.3 and Veo direct prices were not rechecked.

## What would change the answer

- AA or arena.ai adding Kling 4.0 above about 1100. It is already priced at 0.157 USD/s and offers 30 s clips.
- HeyGen confirming a published 768p rate of 0.02 or less, and clean API terms: it would replace Grok Lite for B-roll.
- MiniMax API terms that exclude the US or EU, or grant a licence: that decides whether H3 is the hero or Omni is.
- A pilot of 50 to 100 shots (R4) measuring accepted seconds per model. A model 30% cheaper per generated second but with half the accept rate loses.
