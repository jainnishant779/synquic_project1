# R15 brief: open-weight models to test free on a Kaggle 2x T4

As of 2026-11-02. 80 facts in `raw/R15.jsonl`, all single-source (or marked conflict/unverifiable); none verified by V. Every figure was read through a summarising fetch tool, so V should spot-check quotes. Licence verdicts are for counsel, not legal advice, and I did not open the full licence texts except HunyuanVideo-1.5 and XTTS. "Fits T4" is my reading of vendor VRAM figures and file sizes. **No source gave a T4 or Kaggle timing for any candidate (R15-064); every speed must come from our own runs.** T4 has no native bf16 and no flash-attn 2; most cards are written for bf16, so fp16 numerical behaviour is untested.

## Recommendation

**Notebook A, images (ordered by value)**
1. **FLUX.2 klein 4B**: Apache, not gated, about 13 GB, 4 steps, text-to-image plus multi-reference editing (R15-001 to 004, 032). Unsloth GGUF Q8_0 is 4.3 GB if fp16 is unstable (R15-006). Best first test: it replaces our gated FLUX.1 schnell.
2. **Qwen-Image-Edit-2511** (Apache, 20B, multi-image edit, claims better consistency, no benchmark) (R15-019). Needs GGUF: Q3_K_S 9.27 GB or Q4_K_M 12.8 GB (R15-020), or a split across both T4s. Most promising for "keep the product, change the scene", but the heaviest.
3. **Z-Image-Turbo**: Apache, 6B, vendor says "fits comfortably within 16G", 8 steps, no editing (R15-024, 025). Fast text-to-image baseline.
4. **Sana 1.6B**: Apache, tiny, 20 steps (R15-029). Cheap floor; Gemma encoder terms unread.
Test-only extra: **Qwen-Image-2.1 / Turbo** (7B, unified edit, 8-step Turbo) is under the Qwen Research License, so not for client work until counsel reads it (R15-021, 022).

**Notebook B, video (ordered by value, all need image-to-video)**
1. **Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B**: Apache, T2V+I2V, 720p 24 fps (R15-035). Vendor needs 24 GB (R15-036), so use 480p, fewer frames, offload, tiling. Hub weights are F32, which I suspect (not proven) explains the earlier Wan 2.1 system-RAM failure. 18.3k GitHub stars (R15-037). Fallback: Wan2.2 A14B I2V GGUF Q4_K_M 9.65 GB per expert (R15-039).
2. **Kandinsky 6.0 Video Lite (distilled)**: MIT, 3B, I2V with audio, 10 steps, 864x480, 5 s (R15-055); 1B Full-HD upscaler (R15-057). Newest (about Oct 2026) and the smallest licence-clean option, but no independent quality evidence and component licences unread.
3. **LTX-2.5 distilled GGUF**: I2V, audio, 8+3 steps (R15-047, 050). Gated; free commercial use only under US$10M revenue (R15-046). 22B vs 19B conflict (R15-048). Use UD-Q2_K_XL 9.5 GB or Q4_K_M 13.58 GB through ComfyUI-GGUF; the full diffusers snapshot is 72 GB and will not fit the 20 GB disk (R15-049).
4. **HunyuanVideo-1.5 480p I2V step-distilled**: 8.3B, 14 GB minimum with offload, 8 to 12 steps (R15-043); diffusers I2V example uses float16 (R15-044). Licence excludes EU, UK and South Korea (R15-045). The 7B Qwen2.5-VL encoder is heavy.

## Try-on, TTS, alignment

- Try-on: all three open try-on models are CC BY-NC-SA (R15-065 to 068), so research comparison only. For a licensable route, test klein 4B or Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 with person plus garment references; nobody has measured print fidelity.
- TTS: **Kokoro** (Apache, 82M, has Hindi voices) is the safe offline default (R15-069). **Indic Parler-TTS** (Apache, gated, Hindi) and **IndicF5** (MIT, gated, needs reference clip) are the licensed Hindi options (R15-072, 073). **XTTS-v2 is non-commercial** (R15-070). Piper is GPL-3.0 and Hindi is unconfirmed (R15-074).
- Alignment: **WhisperX** BSD-2 (R15-076) and **faster-whisper** MIT (R15-077); ctc-forced-aligner code is BSD-2 but its default model is CC-BY-NC (R15-078). Hindi support is not confirmed on any README.

## Unknowns and gaps

- No T4 or dual-T4 timing for any model (R15-064). Whether fp16 (not bf16) runs cleanly on klein, Wan 2.2, Kandinsky and LTX is untested.
- Conflicts: LTX-2.5 size 19B/21B/22B (R15-048); Z-Image-Edit existence (R15-026).
- Not read: full licence texts for FLUX/LTX/Qwen Research/Kandinsky and text-encoder licences (Gemma, Llama, Qwen). Kandinsky Pro quote is paraphrased (R15-056).
- Diffusers GGUF loading is documented for Flux only (R15-033); the Wan, Qwen and LTX GGUFs point to ComfyUI-GGUF, so plan one ComfyUI-based notebook cell.
- Not covered: SD 3.5 Large, FLUX.1 Kontext alternatives beyond dev, Hunyuan 1.5 GGUF, Mochi/CogVideoX speed on T4, Hindi alignment models, Indic-Speak.

## What would change the answer

Any first run showing fp16 black images or NaNs on klein/Wan would move GGUF-first. If counsel accepts the FLUX 3 Kommunity or LTX under-US$10M terms, FLUX 3 and LTX-2.5 rise, but FLUX 3 still does not fit a T4 as published. A real Kandinsky 6 I2V sample would decide whether it outranks LTX-2.5.
