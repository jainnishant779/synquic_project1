# R9 brief: images, product and apparel consistency, upscaling

As of 2026-11-01. 136 facts in `raw/R9.jsonl`. All single-source unless marked; nothing is verified by V. Licence verdicts are for counsel, not legal advice. Vendor origin is recorded as a fact (`vendor_origin`) and nothing is excluded on it.

## Recommendation by area

**1. Still frames.** Default to **Nano Banana 2.1**: $0.0336 at 1K, $0.0504 at 2K (R9-028, 029), up to 14 reference images and 9:16 (R9-034, 035), paid tier does not train on prompts (R9-038). It ranks 4th in Artificial Analysis text-to-image (1176) and 3rd-equal in editing (1151) (R9-004, 013). Keep **GPT Image 2** medium ($0.041 portrait, $0.053 square) for text-heavy frames (R9-041, 042); the 2.5 models lead every board but cost $0.21 at max quality (R9-001, 011, 045). Cheapest acceptable: **Muse Image at $0.01** (R9-072) or FLUX 3 Klein at $0.009 (R3-110). Muse is in preview, its price is from a news article, and its terms are unread. Do not trust any model to render a real logo or packaging text; overlay the real asset in post (R9-047, 112).

**2. Independent quality.** Artificial Analysis and Arena agree on the top group (GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1, MAI-Image-2.6) (R9-001 to 027). Elo measures preference, not product accuracy or text. The only independent text-rendering scores are for 2025 models (Nano Banana Pro 85.53, Seedream 4.5 83.83, GPT Image 1.5 82.63) (R9-080 to 082). No independent text score exists for any current model.

**3. Apparel.** Use **FASHN Try-On Max** as the primary: Fast 1K is 1 credit, about $0.075 on-demand (R9-085, 086), no training on customer content, customer owns outputs (R9-089, 090). Cheapest to test: **Nano Banana 2.1 with garment and person references** at $0.0336 (R9-101). Google's try-on (about $0.06, unconfirmed) is discontinued 2027-01-20 (R9-093, 094). FLUX VTO's own docs promise only "minor gains" in print fidelity (R9-096). Print evidence is one unscored run (R9-099); no source tests a T-shirt graphic with text. A pilot on your own printed garments is required.

**4. Product photo to video.** Animate the approved product still as first frame (Kling 4.0 allows first, last and middle frames; Veo and Omni accept image inputs) (R9-103, 106, 107). The only independent benchmark of 18 video models gives text fidelity of 34 or lower out of 100, with distortion under motion (R9-109 to 112). Plan for short, low-motion product shots and overlay logos and text in post. Kling's keyframe mode cannot take element references (R9-103).

**5. Character consistency.** Start with **reference images, not training**. GPT-Image-2 held face best in one paper (0.85 against 0.33 to 0.59 for others) but that is the authors' own benchmark (R9-123). If training is needed, fal FLUX LoRA is $2 per run (R9-117); klein 9B trainer is $0.0043 per step (R9-118). The licences are the problem: klein 9B is non-commercial for self-hosting, 4B is Apache (R9-120, 121). Identity evidence for LoRAs is anecdotal (R9-124).

**6. Upscaling to 1080p.** Cheapest: **ByteDance upscaler, $0.0072/s at 1080p** (R9-128), about $0.43 for 60 s (my arithmetic). Safer for product detail: **Topaz Proteus at $0.02/s** for 720p to 1080p, non-generative (R9-125). Avoid generative models (Starlight family $0.12/s, R9-126) on product shots. SeedVR2 is Apache 2.0 for self-hosting and warns of oversharpening on 720p AI video (R9-131). Real-ESRGAN is BSD-3 and frame-by-frame (R9-132).

## What would change the answer

- A pilot of 30 to 50 real garments and products scored by OCR plus a vision model would replace all fidelity claims here. This is the priority.
- If BFL, OpenAI or Muse terms or the Seedream console price differ from what is recorded, the stills pick changes.
- If Google replaces try-on or Kling publishes try-on terms, the apparel pick changes.
- If founders exclude China-origin vendors, drop the ByteDance upscaler and Seedream. The upscale pick falls to Proteus at $0.02/s, about 3 times the cost.
- If counsel clears fal-hosted FLUX LoRA, training becomes the cheapest identity route.
